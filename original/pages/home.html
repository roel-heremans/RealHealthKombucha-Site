What is Kombucha?

Kombucha is a fermented tea beverage made by fermenting sweetened tea with a symbiotic culture of bacteria and yeast (SCOBY). The fermentation process produces probiotics, enzymes, and organic acids, which contribute to its unique flavor and health benefits.
